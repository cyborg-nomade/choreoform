# RP-01 draft host obligations

**Status:** Synthetic benchmark boundary specification, not implemented support or
execution authorization. The source lowers and passes supported static/control/
protection/invalidation checks and all six later partial static stages. Those
later reports disclose two isolated surviving declarations and one separate
conditional flat owned payment-capture/request/local-classification proof, with
outstanding occurrence ownership/correlation/authority/storage/handoff/settlement
premises. The retained-seed body stays outside legacy reconciliation coverage.
Actual surviving lifetime, progress and irreversible safety remain unproved.
Read [package status](README.md), [manifest](manifest.json) and
[frozen plan](../../evaluation/0034-rp01-package-plan.md).

The exact provider/type/clock bytes define typed boundaries. A matching digest,
nominal invariant, readable role, successful metadata parse or claim field does
not authenticate an actor, receipt, policy, provider fact or live owner.

## Meaning of requests and results

- The check service evaluates the exact fictional policy artifact and checks the
  identified claim against relevant duplicate evidence. `clear`, `correction`,
  `duplicate` and `rejected` are completed check reports, each retaining claim/
  policy revision and reasons. A duplicate finding links another claim and needs
  human resolution. Technical rejection/unknown observations are different from
  a successful report whose business finding is rejected. There is no implemented
  check adapter or live duplicate database in this package.
- The payable command handles one original request with its manager/finance
  witnesses. `paid` requires an actual correlated confirmed payable/payment result
  with the exact amount/currency. `problem` requires a confirmed terminal business
  problem with no unresolved monetary effect hidden behind it. It is not a way to
  turn timeout, acknowledgement or uncertainty into a business failure. A command
  can durably record its decision even when no payable is created; its irreversible
  classification does not imply that every business result debits money.
- Notification has a separately identified command/effect/result. Its result
  must truthfully satisfy the supported notification contract and current recipient
  revision. Payment truth is recorded before notice work; a notice fault must not
  erase confirmed payment or be displayed as proof of payment failure. No real
  message is sent, and no delivery adapter is supplied here.

The exact instance/activity/logical-effect tuple and original request digest form
the technical stable-key boundary. The readable claim ID is not that key and does
not itself deduplicate separate instances. An actual deployment must bind launch
registration/business-identity uniqueness explicitly rather than infer it from
this source or string concatenation.

## Authority and revisions

Roles and the request-submitter relationship must come from authenticated,
instance/revision-bound assignment. They cannot be inferred merely from a claim's
employee string. If any host fact is derived from process data/history, preserve
its actual dependency/protection provenance; do not use a fact to hide a read.
Generic P1 completion checks must bind fresh observation, principal, assignment,
occurrence and exact input revisions. Fields named principal/assignment in a typed
result are claims to verify, not authentication. The current work predicates also
check claim/policy revision, reasons and participant separation.

Employee correction keeps claim identity/employee, creates a different business
revision and atomically requests another cycle. Prior approvals remain recorded;
they cannot authorize the corrected claim. All incoming ordinary paths require
new manager/finance work before payment. The unexpected-stale-evidence fallback
requests no payable and records an explicit problem; it is not renewed approval.
No generic out-of-band mutable-data import API is implemented by this benchmark.

The shared fictional expense envelope explicitly permits the three business
adapters on data and business/clock result labels. Clock observations can affect
payment/control publication, so their downstream recipient circle is explicit too.
This is a deliberately declared authorization circle, not demonstrated least privilege. Payloads separate check/review/payment/
notice information, but that does not prove protected accessible editing, audience
views or all control/timing information flows. Those gates remain open.

## Retry and time

Only the exact eligible fault `confirmed no effect` is selected for another
payment attempt. The host must bind that classification to accepted definitive
no-effect evidence for the exact original request/effect, not to a timeout, HTTP
code, arbitrary caller string or a misleading fault name. The core does not infer
such an emission/classification contract from spelling. The exact fault-source
descriptor now proves the declared static provider/profile/emission binding; actual
authenticated emission and definitive classification remain host obligations.
Current authority, cancellation,
budget, unchanged request/input/basis, stable key and accepted clock stamp/delay
checks still apply. Changed business input requires new work, not a new attempt
using the old key or budget.

The instance-microseconds profile, source contract and input deadline are explicit.
The sample deadline coordinate is a synthetic port value, not an observed current
time or authenticated timer. The actual host must register the exact domain/origin,
authenticate monotonic observations and check overflow, due revision and atomic
fire/cancel. The absolute payment deadline denies pause/extension. Optional
business-calendar reminders are outside this first variant, recorded explicitly.

## Reconciliation and lifetime

The terminal-fact wait envelope has confirmed provider result and correlated
no-effect/rejected/cancelled branches. Authenticated normalization must preserve
original provider evidence, source and observation identity, logical effect,
original request/input/key and occurrence revisions. Its type alone cannot prove
that mapping. Unknown/acknowledged facts must stay pending; stale/duplicate or
contradictory facts must not confirm or settle twice. The true match predicate
operates only after mandatory source/correlation/authority checks and supplies
none of those checks by itself.

The payment recovery now declares fresh local typed evidence capture, a separate
readonly exact original request, and a local receipt-matched category. Its terminal
source descriptor pins the unchanged provider/normalization profile. The original
completion predicate is rebound only to captured confirmed payload and retained
request: claim/revision/amount/currency must match before recording paid. A tag alone
is insufficient; mismatched-evidence preserves the captured payload for accountable
review. Neither that text nor finishing the local body settles a conflict. The
category writes no ordinary payment result, resumes no parent and clears no
cancellation. Original request/capture values require actual accepted immutable
storage and protection for their whole lifetime. Provenance labels in reports
supply no live ancestor value or authority. Matched/no-effect categories grant no
new dispatch or safe technical retry by themselves.

Create each designated surviving follow-up before disposal of its current owner.
There is one capability site per service occurrence; technical retries retain the
same logical effect. This declaration shape does not prove actual obligation or
subscription cardinality. Transfer requires authorized receiver acceptance and
atomic old/new ownership updates. No accepted handoff leaves the original owner
accountable and closure pending. Retain required storage/provenance while any
follow-up needs it; ordinary completion does not make all parent values available.

Ordinary invalidation may classify a private influence as historical only after
its exact synchronous owner occurrence completes ordinary work and no declared
descendant can write that cell. The host must enforce fresh occurrence storage,
value-copy publication and the ordinary-close cutoff. This proves no retained
value availability, current permission, live effect settlement or disposal right;
mutable ancestor inputs remain subject to their own revision/invalidation checks.

The root remaining-owner finish and each follow-up finish are closure gates,
not assertions that obligations disappeared. The actual instance must account
for transitive live children, effects and subscriptions, preserving ordinary
outcomes separately from full terminal disposal. Scope/definition IDs alone are
not live occurrence identities or acceptance evidence. Complete static lifetime/
retention/invalidation/exceptional protection proof remains open; no engine is
implemented to settle these obligations in this package.

Cancellation is a scoped monotonic request and does not reverse payment or prove
that a provider stopped. Atomic accepted ordering during pending review must
prevent new ordinary payment after effective withdrawal, retaining losing/stale
approval observations. If an effect crossed its provider cutoff, its confirmed or
unknown outcome remains owned until explicit settlement; do not report withdrawal
as a confirmed no-effect result. A failed activity/handler is not given an invented
ordinary success continuation.

These obligations are specification requirements for future supported hosts and
proofs. No actual authority, clock, provider, atomic transfer, participant study
or operational observation is supplied. All four scenarios remain Partial.
