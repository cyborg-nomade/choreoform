## Static retention extension

Keep exact local readonly uninitialized result/input-type/source/capture rules and
normal visibility/provider/protection checks. A retained-request scope has either
terminal closure and no lexical children, or reconcile closure and exactly its one
designated direct child. Ordinary creators, fault-handler reuse, ports and arbitrary
extra children remain forbidden. All captures in a body bind its own exact original
source. Static declarations do not initialize data or prove conditional ownership.
