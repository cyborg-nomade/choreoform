cell premise, fresh local initialization and existing ordinary child rules. One
bounded graph/initializer index/budget serves all entries. Immutable references
and conditional coverage do not supply actual activation, storage lifetime,
permissions, obligation ownership, matching provider facts or settlement.

initializer and ordinary-result refusals after link success; fresh local constants
and peer initializer refusal; ordinary and handler roles of the same template;
unused closure metadata without an invented entry; ordinary children and nested
follow-ups; and 401 entries sharing indexes while 400 uncalled owner bodies remain
uncovered. Source tests also record that a follow-up timer's unproved parent basis
refuses at this later stage after successful lowering/links.

cells and separate ordinary coverage, parent-initializer refusal/fresh local
initialization, human/terminal packages without invented entries, and docs/licenses.

remain non-executable even when combined node coverage is complete; coverage is
not protection, lifetime or admission evidence.

semantic/frontend Wasm lint and frontend release Wasm build, and all relevant
artifact/source/stage Python oracles. Logs: `/private/tmp/recon-entry-rust.log`,
